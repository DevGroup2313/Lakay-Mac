WHICH FILE
----------
Both files are in the builds folder of this repository, and on its Releases page
("Lakay for Mac (latest build)", under Assets):

  Lakay-Mac-Apple-Silicon.zip   for Macs with M1, M2, M3, M4 and later (the Mac mini)
  Lakay-Mac-Intel.zip           for Macs with an Intel processor (a 2019 MacBook Pro)

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu, About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M..." is
Apple silicon. "Processor: ... Intel" is Intel.


INSTALL (OR UPDATE)
-------------------
1. Download the file for your Mac. In the builds folder, click the file's name,
   then the download button on the right. Safari unzips it by itself; if it
   does not, double-click the zip. Lakay.app appears in Downloads.
2. Drag Lakay.app into Applications. To update, drag the new one in and choose
   Replace. The store's books stay: they are not kept inside the app.
3. The first time only: double-click Lakay. macOS says it cannot check the app.
   Click Done (not Move to Trash). Open System Settings, Privacy and Security,
   scroll down, click Open Anyway beside Lakay, then Open. On macOS 14 or
   older, you can instead right-click Lakay and choose Open.
   This is asked because Lakay is not signed with a paid Apple certificate.
   It is asked once.

Lakay opens on its door. Enter opens the practice store at once. The little
lock in the card's corner sets up the real store, or signs in to it.


THE STORE'S BOOKS
-----------------
- They are sealed on this Mac, in your own folder:
    ~/Library/Application Support/Emporium
  (Lakay's former name stays on the folder, so nothing is lost.)
- An update keeps them. Removing Lakay.app keeps them too.
- Save a backup in Lakay at closing time: Settings, Save a backup now. The
  file is sealed and opens only with your password or the Master Key. Keep a
  copy on a USB key and another outside the store.
- Each device keeps its own books. To move a store to another computer, save
  a backup here and restore it there.


IF IT DOES NOT OPEN
-------------------
- macOS says Lakay is damaged: delete Lakay.app and the zip, download the zip
  again, and open it with a double-click. If the message stays, note its
  exact words.
- Nothing happens: open Lakay once from Applications, then look in System
  Settings, Privacy and Security for Open Anyway.
- What Lakay noted when it started is in
    ~/Library/Application Support/Emporium/emporium.log
